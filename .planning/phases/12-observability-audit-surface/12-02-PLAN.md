<objective>
Expand the proven read surface sideways: the operator's two highest-frequency
questions become answerable. "Which of my runs needs attention?" is
`dark-factory runs --status WAITING_HUMAN`. "What exactly happened in that
feedback loop, machine-readably?" is `dark-factory events <run> --type
ROLE_BOUNCED --json`.

Purpose: `runs` is the only command that produces a run id without the operator
already knowing one — it is the entry point to every other command in the set,
which is why its id column must survive rendering intact. `--json` / `--type`
complete D-05's locked `events` contract.

Output: `read_run_list`, `RunListRow`, `render_runs_table`, the `runs` command,
and the `--json` / `--type` options on `events`.

> For any run, Dark Factory can say exactly where it is and retell everything that
> happened to it — the observability half of the durability thesis.
</objective>
