files" — is emitted by the orchestrator, persisted by the one mutation path, read
back by a new read-model module, redacted, rendered, and printed by a new
`dark-factory events <run-id>` command.

`run_events` → read model → redaction → `rich` render → Typer command → exit code)
works end-to-end before any horizontal expansion. Every later plan in this phase
builds out from this proven slice.

Output: `EventType.ARTIFACT_CREATED` emitted and read; `events/reader.py`,
`observability/render.py`, `EventRow`; the `events` command; two architectural
guards.
